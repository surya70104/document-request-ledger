Retention & DPDP

Every stored file carries a retention basis and a purge date. Evidence is kept for the audit-file period; what has no basis to be kept goes on schedule and is erased - with a permanent record that it existed.

SA 230 and the NFRA audit-file circular require seven years; the DPDP Rules 2025 (substantive obligations from 12 May 2027) require erasure once the purpose is served. Retention required by law is a recognised basis, so the firm must show which basis each document is held on.
